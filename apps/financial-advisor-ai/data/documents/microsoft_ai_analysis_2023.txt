
    Microsoft Corporation (MSFT) Annual Report 2023
    
    Microsoft delivered strong financial performance in fiscal year 2023 with
    revenue of $211.9 billion, representing 7% growth year over year.
    
    Artificial Intelligence Integration:
    Microsoft has positioned itself as a leader in the AI boom through its partnership
    with OpenAI and integration of AI capabilities across its product suite.
    
    Key AI Initiatives:
    - Copilot integration across Office 365 and Windows
    - Azure OpenAI Service for enterprise customers
    - GitHub Copilot for developers
    - Bing Chat powered by GPT-4
    
    Cloud Services Growth:
    Azure and other cloud services revenue grew 27% year over year.
    Microsoft 365 Commercial products revenue increased 11%.
    
    Segment Performance:
    - Productivity and Business Processes: $69.3 billion (+9%)
    - Intelligent Cloud: $87.9 billion (+16%)
    - More Personal Computing: $54.7 billion (-9%)
