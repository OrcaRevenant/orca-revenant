
    Apple Inc. (AAPL) Q4 2023 Financial Results
    
    Apple reported record Q4 2023 revenue of $89.5 billion, up 1% year over year.
    iPhone revenue was $43.8 billion, down 3% from the previous year.
    Services revenue reached a new all-time high of $22.3 billion, up 16% year over year.
    
    Key Financial Metrics:
    - Gross margin: 45.2%
    - Operating margin: 30.1%
    - Net income: $22.96 billion
    - Earnings per share: $1.46
    
    The company returned over $24 billion to shareholders during the quarter through
    dividends and share repurchases. Apple's cash and marketable securities totaled
    $162.1 billion at the end of the quarter.
